Important partner, rate & regulatory disclosure

Nexa Capital is an authorized Direct Selling Agent (DSA) / Channel Partner operating in partnership with bank-accredited networks (including Ruconnect). We do not directly lend money; all loans are approved and disbursed by partner Banks/NBFCs. Loan offers remain subject to the relevant partner lender’s independent assessment, eligibility rules, KYC, approval, terms and disbursal process. Nexa Capital does not guarantee approval or funding.

Rates starting from 10.49% p.a., “zero upfront fees”, digital processing, any 24-hour timeline, partner names, ratings, amounts and testimonials displayed on this concept are illustrative and must be verified before use. Rates, interest, fees and other terms vary by lender and customer profile; review the lender’s Key Facts Statement and loan agreement before acceptance. Any fee charged by a bank/NBFC must be disclosed by that lender.

Personal information should be collected only for a clear purpose, with informed consent, and shared only with relevant parties as permitted by applicable law. When configured, this prototype’s notification backend forwards the submitted lead fields to Nexa’s nominated email and WhatsApp recipients; it does not save them in a lead database or connect to a lender or credit bureau. Do not enable collection until the privacy notice, retention policy and consent wording are approved.

Live digital lending journeys must comply with applicable Indian law and RBI requirements, including the Reserve Bank of India (Digital Lending) Directions, 2025, as amended or replaced. No RBI or government endorsement is implied.

Grievance redressal: Before launch, publish Nexa Capital’s grievance officer name, contact information and escalation timeline, along with each lending partner’s applicable grievance mechanism. Contact details must be completed before going live.

Launch checklist: independently verify Nexa Capital’s DSA/channel-partner authorisations, Ruconnect relationship, each lender’s permission to be displayed, all pricing/fee claims, and customer-review evidence before publishing this copy.
