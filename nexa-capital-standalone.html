Important partner, rate & regulatory disclosure

Nexa Capital is an authorized Direct Selling Agent (DSA) / Channel Partner operating in partnership with bank-accredited networks (including Ruconnect). We do not directly lend money; all loans are approved and disbursed by partner Banks/NBFCs. Loan offers remain subject to the relevant partner lender’s independent assessment, eligibility rules, KYC, approval, terms and disbursal process. Nexa Capital does not guarantee approval or funding.

Rates starting from 9.99% p.a. are indicative, not a personalised offer. Any zero-upfront-fee or processing-time claim is subject to the applicable lender and service terms. Rates, interest, fees and other terms vary by lender and customer profile; review the lender’s Key Facts Statement and loan agreement before acceptance. Any fee charged by a bank/NBFC must be disclosed by that lender.

Personal information should be collected only for a clear purpose, with informed consent, and shared only with relevant parties as permitted by applicable law. When configured, this site’s enquiry backend forwards the submitted lead fields to Nexa’s nominated email and WhatsApp recipients; it does not save them in a lead database or connect to a lender or credit bureau. Do not enable collection until the privacy notice, retention policy and consent wording are approved.

Live digital lending journeys must comply with applicable Indian law and RBI requirements, including the Reserve Bank of India (Digital Lending) Directions, 2025, as amended or replaced. No RBI or government endorsement is implied.

Grievance redressal: Before launch, publish Nexa Capital’s grievance officer name, contact information and escalation timeline, along with each lending partner’s applicable grievance mechanism. Contact details must be completed before going live.

Lender names do not imply endorsement. Direct partner relationships, DSA/channel-partner authorisations, product availability and pricing must be independently verified.
